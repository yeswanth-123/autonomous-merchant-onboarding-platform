Privacy & retention policy

What we store and how long we keep it.

What we store

Account
Name, email and a salted scrypt password hash.
Application data
Business, PAN, GSTIN and bank details: encrypted field by field.
Documents
Your uploads: encrypted (AES-256-GCM) with a unique key per file.
Audit trail
Who did what and when, with identifiers masked, never your documents.
Third parties
Verification providers receive only the values needed for each check. Documents are never sent to outside services.

How long we keep it

Rejected / expired / withdrawn
Documents deleted after {{ retention.get('terminal_documents_days', 30) }} days; record anonymised after {{ retention.get('terminal_anonymise_days', 90) }} days.
Merchants
Kept while your merchant account is live. Once it ends, kept for the legal retention period ({{ ((retention.get('merchant_retention_days', 2555) or 0) / 365)|round(1) }} years), then anonymised.
Sessions
Sign-in sessions expire after 12 hours (2 hours idle).
